### About Me
I am a QA Enthusiast who thrives on the combination of meticulous manual testing and clear, actionable technical documentation. I have built a solid foundation in crafting structured test plans, detailed bug reports, and comprehensive test cases that keep cross-functional teams aligned. Right now, I am curating a portfolio that not only showcases my established expertise in manual testing but also highlights my active transition into the world of automation. I am currently learning and building projects around web and mobile automation testing, focusing on how to effectively bridge the gap between rigorous manual scenarios and modern automated scripts.
